## Attunement + item-granted weapon proficiency (owner's ruling, 2026-10-08)

- **Rule:** an item that needs attunement but isn't attuned keeps its plain +N enchantment (a Sword +2 stays +2, Full Plate +2 keeps +2 AC) but grants NONE of its special effects. `engine/rules/5e/attunement.js` `applyAttunement` strips the effect fields; the sheet's one equipped-items boundary (`dnd._equippedOnly`) applies it, so every stat/AC/check/attack sees it.
- **Item-granted weapon proficiency:** new item field `grants_weapon_proficiency`; `isProficientWithWeapon` takes the equipped items. Bracers of Archery (longbow + shortbow) wired in `item-mechanics.json` — Sorra and Siv wear them.
- **What it changes right now** (equipped, needs attunement, `attuned: false` in the data): Iyani AC 14->12 and spell DC 19->17 (Bracers of Defense, The Lexicon); Lyria AC 14->12 and DC 19->17 (Bracers of Defense, Staff of Power); Ferghus STR 21->18 (Belt of Giant Strength); Rith AC 12->10 (Bracers of Defense); also off: Ferghus's Bracers of Swiftness, Lyria's Robe of Stars. **If any of these are actually attuned, set `attuned: true` on the item** (Items screen).
