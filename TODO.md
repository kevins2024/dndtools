- [ ] **House rule: a natural 20 on initiative earns a reroll point (project owner's idea, 2026-11-04).** A natural 20 on an initiative roll still gives the character their initiative score as normal, and also gives them one point (luck / inspiration) they can spend to reroll something during that fight; it is gone when the fight ends. Not RAW: in 2024 a natural 20 on an Initiative check (a Dexterity check) does nothing special. Engine side: `combatTurn.js` (it owns initiative and fight state) records the natural die and grants a fight-scoped point; spending it should reuse the Heroic Inspiration reroll (`d20Test.js` rerolls, `heroic_inspiration` on the character) so there is one reroll mechanic, not two. Open questions: does it stack with Heroic Inspiration the character already holds (probably a separate point, so both can exist), and does it reroll any die (like Heroic Inspiration) or only d20 tests?
