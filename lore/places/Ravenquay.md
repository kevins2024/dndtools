# Ravenquay

River delta city, home base for the Dawn Blades. One day's sail south of
[[Trevash]], three days south of [[Khemrise]].

## Culture / Notes

A river delta city where commerce and underworld politics are inseparable
from each other and from the docks. People arrive here looking for a fresh
start or a fast deal — often the same thing. Busy, layered, and never fully
asleep.
