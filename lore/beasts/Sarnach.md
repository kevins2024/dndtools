# Sarnach

A pale, heat-shimmer-colored raptor native only to the [[Orvath (Region)|Orvath region]]
and nowhere else — larger than any lowland eagle, and the animal the
region's entire falconry culture (and, indirectly, its banking economy)
is built around.

## Hunting and bonding

Hunts by reading thermals and mirage-distortion most creatures can't parse
at all — a sense tied to the region's harsh day-to-night temperature swing
and thin, dry air, not something that transplants to a lowland raptor.
Clans bond one sarnach per generation per family, sometimes for life; a
bonded pair is a serious, rare thing, not a casual working relationship.

## Why it actually matters

A trained sarnach isn't valuable primarily as a hunting bird. Its real
worth is that it can spot a distant desert storm rolling in long before any
human eye can — and in Orvath, a storm means a fresh strike of
**duskglass** (lightning-fused sand with a latent magical-anchoring
property found nowhere else) somewhere out in the waste, up for grabs to
whichever clan gets there first. A clan's standing, wealth, and leverage
over the settled city is downstream of how good their sarnach is. See
[[Orvath (Region)]] for the full duskglass economy this feeds.
