# Character ability-score / feat-provenance audit checklist

Started 2026-09-09, prompted directly by a real mistake found the same day: Lenn's
"Empowered Spell" was wrongly deleted as "mechanically impossible for a pure Wizard"
during the subclass audit, without checking whether a feat (Metamagic Adept) legally
granted it. That raised the obvious follow-up question — how many other characters
have a feature that's only explicable by a feat that isn't actually listed on their
sheet, or an ability score that doesn't reconcile under this campaign's real
character-creation method?

This file exists for the same reason `engine/SUBCLASS_AUDIT.md` does: so progress
survives an interruption, and so nobody re-checks a character that's already clean.

## What "checked" means here — two specific things per character

1. **Ability scores reconcile.** This campaign uses standard 27-point buy
   (`NewCharacterTool.vue`'s `POINT_BUY_COSTS`/`POINT_BUY_BUDGET`, base scores 8-15).
   For each character: back out their `ability_score_history` entries (racial
   bonuses, ASIs, feat-granted +1s) from their current `stat_*` values, and confirm
   what's left is a legal 27-point-buy spend in the 8-15 range. A history entry that
   just says "Player's choice" or a flat "+2/+1" with no species-specific bonus
   named is a red flag — flag it and pin down which two abilities it actually was
   for that species (own reasoning is fine only if it's the _only_ legal
   explanation; otherwise ask). Real racial ability bonuses (2014 PHB/Xanathar's/
   Tasha's custom-lineage rules) should be visible and correct for the character's
   actual `race`/`subrace`.
2. **Feat-granted features have their feat.** Any ability that's normally a feat's
   effect (War Caster's opportunity-attack spellcasting, Ritual Caster's extra
   rituals, Actor's Disguise Self mimicry, Fey Touched's Misty Step + 1 spell,
   Telekinetic's Mage Hand, Metamagic Adept's Metamagic pool, Magic Initiate's
   cantrip+1st-level-spell, Shadow Touched, Telepathic, etc.) needs the feat itself
   present in `features` — not just the granted effect floating free with no
   explanation. If the feat is missing, check whether it's genuinely absent (bug,
   fix it) or the ability came from somewhere else entirely (race, subclass,
   background) that just wasn't obvious at a glance.

Scope explicitly does NOT include (that's the bigger, later "full rebuild" TODO
item): spells known/prepared counts, HP totals, skill proficiency counts, spell
slot counts, or anything else level-up math would normally derive — just ability
scores and feat provenance for now.
