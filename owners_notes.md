Petra is not crazy and she is not nonsensical. She is simply operating on a different emotional register than the situation calls for, and she's completely sincere about it. The humor comes from the gap between what the moment demands and what she provides.
In danger she may be calm. In calm she may be intense. In grief she may find something worth noting that isn't grief. She doesn't ignore what's happening — she just refuses to be governed by the expected emotional response to it.
She takes words literally when others mean them figuratively. She responds to the subtext of a situation rather than the text. She arrives at correct conclusions through routes that make no apparent sense. She says the thing everyone was thinking but would never say, framed in a way that makes it sound like she's talking about something else entirely.
She is never performing. That's the key. Everything she says she means completely. The comedy and the insight are both accidental byproducts of genuine sincerity.
What she is not: she does not talk to inanimate objects as though they have inner lives. She does not make random non-sequiturs. She is not a quirky NPC generator. She is a person who sees things sideways and reports what she sees with complete honesty.
